# Keine Zeit RoleSwap

**No more right-clicking your portrait.** WoW Forever often sets your group role to damage, even if you are specced as a tank. Keine Zeit RoleSwap sets the role you choose automatically, every time you join a group.

## Features
- **Automatic role** when you join a group or the group changes
- **Answers role checks** from the group leader for you
- **Saved per character**: tank on your warrior, healer on your priest
- **Minimap button** with a small menu to switch your role, drag it around the minimap
- Lightweight, no dependencies

## Requirements
- WoW Forever

## Installation
1. Download the latest release
2. Unzip into `World of Warcraft\_forever_\Interface\AddOns\`
3. Make sure the folder is named `KeineZeitRoleSwap`

## Usage
Click the minimap button and pick **Tank**, **DD**, **Heiler** or **Aus**.

Or use the slash commands:
- `/roleswap tank`
- `/roleswap dd`
- `/roleswap heal`
- `/roleswap aus` turns it off for this character
- `/roleswap` shows your current setting

`/rolle` works the same way. The default role is tank.
